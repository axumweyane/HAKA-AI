## Step 1: Trade Name Registration

**Office:** Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (MoTRI) or local Trade Bureau office.

### Checklist
- [ ] Choose trade name: **"HAKA Security Consulting"** (have 2–3 alternatives ready)
- [ ] Verify name availability at MoTRI name search desk
- [ ] Fill Trade Name Registration Application Form (available at MoTRI)
- [ ] Bring:
  - 2 passport-sized photos (4 cm × 3 cm, white background)
  - Copy of national ID / Kebele ID / passport
- [ ] Pay name registration fee: **~500–1,000 ETB**
- [ ] Receive: Trade Name Registration Certificate

### Notes
- Name must not be identical or confusingly similar to any existing registered business.
- Name must reflect the nature of the business (avoid names suggesting government affiliation, banking, or insurance unless licensed).
- The certificate is valid for a set period (typically renewable).

### Timeline: 1–3 business days

---

## Step 2: TIN (Tax Identification Number)

**Office:** Ministry of Revenues (MoR)

### Checklist
- [ ] After trade name is approved, MoTRI typically forwards your details to MoR
- [ ] If not automatic, visit the nearest MoR branch office
- [ ] Bring:
  - Trade Name Registration Certificate
  - Copy of national ID
- [ ] TIN is issued immediately — no fee
- [ ] Receive: TIN Certificate (8-digit number for individuals, 10-digit for businesses)

### Notes
- TIN is required before you can get your business license.
- You need the TIN for: invoicing, bank account opening, and tax filings.
- As a sole proprietor, your personal TIN may serve double duty — confirm at MoR.

### Timeline: Same day (often issued with business license)

---

## Step 3: Business License

**Office:** Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (MoTRI)

### Checklist
- [ ] Return to MoTRI (or local Trade Bureau) after trade name approval
- [ ] Present:
  - Trade Name Registration Certificate
  - TIN Certificate
  - Copy of national ID
  - 2 additional passport photos
- [ ] Declare business activity: **"IT and Professional Services — Cybersecurity Consulting"**
- [ ] Pay business license fee: **~2,000–5,000 ETB** (varies by category and capital)
- [ ] Receive: **Business License Certificate**

### Category Classification
- Cybersecurity consulting falls under **"IT Services / Professional Services"** in Ethiopian trade classification.
- No special NBE or INSA license is required for general cybersecurity consulting (only for cryptographic equipment or classified government work — see Step 6).

### Timeline: 1–5 business days (after name registration)

## Step 4: Bank Account (Dashen Bank)

**Bank:** Dashen Bank (preferred)

### Checklist
- [ ] Visit any Dashen Bank branch
- [ ] Request business account opening form (sole proprietorship)
- [ ] Bring:
  - Original + copy of **Business License**
  - Original + copy of **TIN Certificate**
  - National ID / passport (original + copy)
  - Trade Name Registration Certificate (bring it)
- [ ] Minimum deposit: typically **0–500 ETB** (confirm at branch)
- [ ] Account setup time: **1–3 business days**
- [ ] Receive: Account number, checkbook (if requested), debit card

### Notes
- You may want a **USD account** if you plan to invoice international clients.
- Dashen Bank has experience with IT sector clients.
- Mobile banking and online banking available — set these up.

### Timeline: 1–3 business days

## Step 5: VAT Registration (Optional)

**Office:** Ministry of Revenues (MoR) — VAT Department

### When Required
- Mandatory if **annual taxable revenue exceeds 1,000,000 ETB** (~$8,000 at current rates).
- Below this threshold, you are **exempt** and should NOT register.

### If You Must Register:
- [ ] File VAT registration application at MoR
- [ ] Present:
  - Business License
  - TIN Certificate
  - Bank account details
- [ ] Receive: VAT Registration Certificate
- [ ] VAT rate: **15%** on taxable supplies (services)
- [ ] Filing: Monthly VAT returns (by the 30th of the following month)

### Practical Advice
- **Do not register for VAT prematurely.** As a new consultancy, you will likely be below the 1M ETB threshold.
- Once registered, you must charge 15% VAT on every invoice, file monthly returns, and issue proper VAT invoices.
- You can register voluntarily, but it adds compliance burden.
- **Recommendation:** Start without VAT registration. Register only when approaching the threshold or when required by a client (some institutions require VAT-registered vendors).

### Timeline: 3–7 business days (if registering)

## Step 6: NBE Notification (If Working with Banks)

**Office:** National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) — Financial Supervision Directorate

### When Required
- When providing security assessment services to **NBE-regulated financial institutions** (banks, insurance companies, microfinance institutions).
- NBE may require notification or registration as a cybersecurity service provider for the financial sector.

### Checklist
- [ ] Research NBE Directive FIS/01/2021 and SBB/77/2020 for cybersecurity service provider requirements
- [ ] Contact NBE Financial Supervision Directorate for current requirements:
  - Address: Sudan Street, Addis Ababa
  - Phone: +251 11 551 7430
- [ ] Prepare documentation:
  - Business License
  - TIN Certificate
  - Professional qualifications/certifications
  - Description of services offered
  - Any relevant cybersecurity certifications (CISSP, CEH, OSCP, etc.)
- [ ] Check if any **capacity assessment** or **due diligence** process is required

### Notes
- This is the step with the most regulatory uncertainty. NBE has been developing cybersecurity supervision frameworks.
- **INSA (Information Network Security Agency)** regulates certain cybersecurity activities — but primarily for government/defense, not private consulting.
- For general vulnerability assessments and penetration testing of private financial institutions, standard business registration should suffice — but always confirm directly with NBE before starting an engagement.

### Timeline: Ongoing — verify before each banking engagement
